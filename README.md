# ScamWatch

ScamWatch is a community-driven platform for checking suspicious contacts, reporting scams, and helping others avoid fraud.

## What You Can Do

- Check a phone number, email, website, or UPI ID against community reports.
- Report scam incidents with descriptions, contact details, locations, and evidence.
- Browse and search reported scams by category and status.
- Confirm reports and add helpful comments.
- View risk scores and related reports that may involve the same scammer.
- Explore reported incidents on the hotspot map.
- Subscribe to email or SMS alerts for phone numbers and UPI IDs you are monitoring.
- View case progress and investigation updates on report pages.

## Using ScamWatch

1. Open **Quick Scam Check** on the home page and search a suspicious contact.
2. Review the risk score, matching reports, reported loss, categories, and statuses.
3. Create an account to report a scam, confirm a report, comment, or receive alerts.
4. Use **Hotspot Map** to explore reports with pinned locations.
5. Open any report to review its evidence, related cases, comments, and status history.

## Alerts

Signed-in users can enable email or SMS alerts after checking a phone number or UPI ID. Alerts help notify you when active matching reports are available.

## Responsible Use

- Verify information independently before taking action.
- Do not share passwords, OTPs, banking details, or private documents in reports or comments.
- Submit only truthful information and evidence you have the right to share.
- Do not use reports to harass, threaten, or identify private individuals.
- ScamWatch information is community-submitted and should not replace advice from banks, law enforcement, or legal professionals.

## Privacy and Security

ScamWatch is designed to protect account credentials and restrict administrative operations. Do not publish personal credentials, private configuration, service tokens, or internal deployment information in public issues, screenshots, or reports.

For help or to report incorrect information, use the support channel provided by the ScamWatch service administrator.
